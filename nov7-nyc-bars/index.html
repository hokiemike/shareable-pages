How this was put together

  • Availability comes from Resy's live search for a party of 2 on Nov 7, plus OpenTable for Nubeluz. Checked Mon Oct 26, 2026. A half-hour shows as open if any table was free in it (5:00 covers 5:00 and 5:15).
  • Walk times are estimates along the street grid from Fifth Ave & 36th St at about 3 mph.
  • Ratings are Resy diner ratings out of 5, with the number of ratings.
  • The word combines critic coverage (Time Out, The Infatuation, Resy's editors) with the general tone of r/cocktails, r/FoodNYC, and r/AskNYC threads. Reddit couldn't be read live, so treat those calls as a read of the consensus, not quotes.
